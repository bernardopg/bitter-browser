Bitter orange marmalade

Seville oranges, sugar and patience. Bright, sharp, and never too sweet.

2 h 30 min6 jarsEasy

Ingredients

  • 1 kg Seville oranges
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1.6 kg sugar
  • 2.5 L water

Method

  1. Halve the oranges and squeeze out the juice and pips.
  2. Slice the peel thinly and simmer for two hours until soft.
