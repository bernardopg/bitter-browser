Native web browser for Linux

The web, unsweetened.

Bitter Browser takes out the trackers, the ads and the noise, and keeps your tabs in a quiet sidebar. Built in Rust on GTK 4 and WebKitGTK, it feels at home on GNOME, KDE Plasma and every Linux desktop.

All install options Download a package

Nothing added

Everything you need, nothing you didn't ask for.

Ads and trackers blocked

EasyList and EasyPrivacy filters on by default, with a per-site switch when a page needs it.

Tabs in a sidebar

Vertical tabs in two workspaces, each with its own order and selection. Session restore loads only what you look at.

HTTPS-only mode

Upgrade every connection, block third-party cookies, and ask before sites use your camera, microphone or location.

Native to your desktop

GTK 4 and libadwaita, light and dark schemes, Wayland and X11. One click makes it your default browser.

Fast to start, easy to read

Rust and WebKitGTK, local suggestions from history and bookmarks, find in page, zoom per site, full screen.

Open source

Mozilla Public License 2.0. Signed releases with checksums, an SBOM and build provenance for every file.
