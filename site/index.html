Privacy by default

Blocked before they load.

EasyList and EasyPrivacy run from the first page, cross-site tracking prevention and third-party cookie blocking are on, and HTTPS-only mode is one switch away. When a site needs an exception, it is one click in the address bar.

  • Ad and tracker blocking
  • HTTPS-only mode
  • Per-site permissions
  • Clear data in one step

Command palette

Everything, one keystroke away.

Press Ctrl K and type. Open, close and reorder tabs, find in the page, bookmark, zoom, clear data or change site permissions without reaching for the mouse.

  • Fuzzy search
  • Shortcuts shown inline
  • Keyboard first

Two workspaces

Work and life, apart.

Tabs stack in a vertical sidebar with full titles and site icons. Main and Second keep their own order and selection, and the session comes back exactly as you left it — loading only the tab you look at.

  • Vertical tabs
  • Session restore
  • Reopen closed tabs

Local first

Your history stays yours.

History, bookmarks and suggestions live in a local database on your disk. The address bar suggests from them as you type; nothing is sent to a server to be remembered.

  • Instant suggestions
  • Editable bookmarks
  • Remove any entry

Native to Linux

Feels at home on your desktop.

GTK 4 and libadwaita, light and dark schemes, Wayland and X11. It starts fast, follows your system fonts and colors, and becomes your default browser with a single click.

  • KDE Plasma
  • Xfce
  • Cinnamon
