Brand and media kit

A drop of bitters.

The mark is a lowercase b drawn as a drop of aromatic bitters: one circle, one point, two tangents. Bitterness is the taste of things nobody sweetened to please you, which is the whole idea of the browser.

Voice

The web, unsweetened.

Tagline: Be bitter. Browse better. Short sentences, concrete facts, a dry wit used sparingly. Campaign lines: Zero added trackers. No sugar-coated ads. Taste the real web. Less sugar, more web.

Press

In fifty words.

Bitter Browser is an open-source web browser for Linux, written in Rust with GTK 4, libadwaita and WebKitGTK. It keeps tabs in a vertical sidebar organized in two workspaces, blocks ads and trackers by default, and installs on Arch, Debian, Ubuntu, Fedora and any Flatpak system. The web, unsweetened.
